About the Snowpiercer Quiz

The Snowpiercer Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge for fans of the television series Snowpiercer. It tests how well you remember the characters, factions, train systems, locations and major events that shape the story after the world is devastated by the Freeze.

The questions cover major characters including Andre Layton, Melanie Cavill and Mr. Wilford, along with Alexandra Cavill and other important figures. The quiz also explores Snowpiercer, Big Alice, the Tailies, the Breachmen, the Freeze, New Eden and important events throughout the series.

Some questions focus on major characters and events, while others test details that dedicated viewers may remember from specific episodes. Whether you watched Snowpiercer for its post-apocalyptic world, its characters or the struggle for control of the train, this quiz gives you a chance to test your knowledge.

How the Snowpiercer Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and choose the answer you believe is correct before moving forward.

Each correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 50 questions in total, the maximum possible score is 100 points. Your final score is used to determine your rank.

The questions cover details from all four seasons, giving the quiz a broad range of subjects. A high score means you remember many of the characters, factions, locations and events aboard Snowpiercer and beyond, while a lower score may mean there are still some details left to rediscover.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Choose the answer you believe is correct and continue through the quiz.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review your previous selection. Your current question number and progress are displayed while you play.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until you have gone through all 50 questions. Once you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and Snowpiercer rank.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and challenge other fans.

Snowpiercer Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

What does the quiz cover?

Questions cover Layton, Melanie Cavill, Mr. Wilford, Alexandra Cavill, Snowpiercer, Big Alice, the Tailies, the Breachmen, the Freeze, New Eden and major events from the series.

Does this quiz cover all four seasons?

Yes. The quiz includes characters, events and details from all four seasons of Snowpiercer.

What does my score mean?

Your score determines your final rank, ranging from a lower-level result such as Frozen Casualty to Snowpiercer-expert-level knowledge.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to replay the quiz and try for a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
