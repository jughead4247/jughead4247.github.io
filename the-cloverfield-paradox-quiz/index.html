About The Cloverfield Paradox Movie Quiz

The Cloverfield Paradox Movie Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge based on the 2018 science-fiction horror film The Cloverfield Paradox. The story follows Ava Hamilton and an international crew aboard Cloverfield Station as they attempt to activate the Shepard particle accelerator during an energy crisis on Earth.

The movie combines science fiction, space survival and horror as the crew deals with the consequences of the accelerator experiment. Once the experiment produces an unexpected result, the crew discovers that something is seriously wrong aboard the station.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, locations, technology, unusual events and major story details. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember what happened aboard Cloverfield Station.

The Cloverfield Paradox Movie Details

Release year: 2018

Director: Julius Onah

Genre: Science fiction, horror and thriller

Main setting: Cloverfield Station, a space station orbiting Earth

Main character: Ava Hamilton, played by Gugu Mbatha-Raw

Key technology: The Shepard particle accelerator

Story connection: The film is connected to the wider Cloverfield universe.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in The Cloverfield Paradox. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of specific events aboard the station.

Characters

Questions may cover Ava Hamilton, Ernst Schmidt, Monk Acosta, Kiel, Mundy, Volkov, Tam, Jensen, Michael and other characters who play important roles in the story.

Cloverfield Station

The quiz includes details about the space station, the crew's situation after the experiment and different problems that develop as the story progresses.

The Shepard Accelerator

The Shepard particle accelerator is central to the movie's story. Questions can test your memory of why the crew is operating the accelerator and what happens after the experiment succeeds.

Strange Events and Anomalies

The crew encounters a series of unexplained and dangerous events after the experiment. The quiz covers several of these events, including unusual changes aboard the station and the consequences faced by the crew.

The Alternate Reality

The movie explores the consequences of the accelerator experiment and introduces events involving another reality. Questions may test your memory of how the alternate reality connects with the events aboard the station.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 30 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, continue to the next question.

Finishing the Quiz

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers The Cloverfield Paradox, including Ava Hamilton, Schmidt, Acosta, Kiel, Mundy, Volkov, Tam, Jensen, the Shepard accelerator, Cloverfield Station, strange anomalies, alternate realities and major story events.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover the Shepard experiment, Cloverfield Station, unusual events, technology, locations and important plot details.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the 2018 science-fiction horror film The Cloverfield Paradox, directed by Julius Onah and starring Gugu Mbatha-Raw as Ava Hamilton.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 30 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
