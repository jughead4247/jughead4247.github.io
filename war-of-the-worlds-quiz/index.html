About The War of the Worlds Movie Quiz

The War of the Worlds Movie Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge based on the science-fiction disaster film War of the Worlds. The story follows Ray Ferrier and his children, Rachel and Robbie, as they struggle to survive a sudden and devastating alien invasion.

The movie combines science fiction, disaster, action and survival as humanity faces enormous alien machines known as tripods. As the invasion spreads, Ray attempts to protect his children while searching for safety.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, alien invasion, tripods, locations, major events, survival situations and important story details. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember what happened during the invasion.

War of the Worlds Movie Details

Release year: 2005

Director: Steven Spielberg

Genre: Science fiction, disaster and thriller

Main setting: New York and the northeastern United States during the invasion

Main character: Ray Ferrier, played by Tom Cruise

Main threat: Alien tripods attacking humanity

Story basis: The film is based on H. G. Wells' 1898 novel The War of the Worlds.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in War of the Worlds. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of specific events during the alien invasion.

Characters

Questions may cover Ray Ferrier, Rachel Ferrier, Robbie Ferrier, Mary Ann, Tim, Ogilvy and other characters who play important roles in the story.

The Alien Invasion

The quiz includes details about the beginning of the invasion, the destruction caused by the tripods and the Ferrier family's attempts to escape the advancing threat.

The Tripods

The alien tripods are central to the movie's conflict. Questions can test your memory of their appearance, weapons, behavior and role during the invasion.

Survival and Escape

Ray and his children encounter numerous dangerous situations while trying to find safety. The quiz covers several of these survival situations and the decisions made by the characters.

Major Story Events

The quiz also covers important events throughout the movie, including the invasion, the family's journey, encounters with other survivors and the final stages of the conflict.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 30 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, continue to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 30 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers War of the Worlds, including Ray Ferrier, Rachel, Robbie, the alien invasion, tripods, the military response, survival situations and major story events.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover the alien invasion, tripods, locations, survival situations, technology and important plot details.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the 2005 science-fiction disaster film War of the Worlds, directed by Steven Spielberg and starring Tom Cruise as Ray Ferrier.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 30 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.

Spoiler Warning
