What Are Apocalypse Quizzes?

Apocalypse Quizzes is a collection of fictional decision-making experiences built around survival, zombies, disasters, and the difficult choices people might face when normal society disappears.

These quizzes are designed for entertainment. Your decisions determine your results, and different choices can lead to very different outcomes.

What Will You Have to Decide?

Depending on the test, you may have to decide how to manage limited resources, choose where to shelter, assess risks, deal with strangers, protect your group, and balance survival against helping others.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these real survival tests?

No. These are fictional entertainment experiences. They are not designed to predict how someone would perform during a real emergency or disaster.

Are the quiz results scientifically accurate?

No. The scoring systems are created for entertainment and are not professional psychological, personality, military, or survival assessments.

Can I take the quizzes more than once?

Yes. Try different decisions and see how your results change.

Will there be more quizzes?

Yes. The website is designed to grow into a collection of fictional apocalypse and survival quizzes.
