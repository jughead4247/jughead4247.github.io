ABOUT THE SITE

About Apocalypse Quizzes

Apocalypse Quizzes is a collection of fictional decision-making experiences based around zombies, survival, disasters, and other apocalypse scenarios.

The quizzes place you in hypothetical situations and ask you to decide what you would do. Your choices can affect your final results.

Our Quizzes

The website currently features zombie survival quizzes with different styles, lengths, and scoring systems. More fictional apocalypse quizzes may be added in the future.

Designed for Entertainment

Apocalypse Quizzes is intended for entertainment and interactive storytelling. The results are fictional and should not be considered predictions of real-world survival ability or personal characteristics.

Different Choices, Different Results

There is not always one obvious answer to a scenario. Some decisions may prioritize survival, while others may involve helping people, managing resources, accepting risks, or protecting a group.

Future Quizzes

The website may expand with additional fictional scenarios involving different types of disasters, survival situations, and difficult decisions.
