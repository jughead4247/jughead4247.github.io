ABOUT APOCALYPSE QUIZZES

About Apocalypse Quizzes

Apocalypse Quizzes is a collection of fictional decision-making experiences built around zombies, survival, disasters, and hypothetical situations where normal society has broken down.

Each quiz places you in different scenarios and asks what you would actually do. Your choices can affect your final result, and different decisions can lead to very different outcomes.

Our Quizzes

Zombie Apocalypse Quiz

Our original zombie survival quiz puts you through fictional situations involving the initial outbreak, shelter, resources, danger, and other survivors. The goal is to see how your decisions affect your survival outcome.

Take the Zombie Apocalypse Quiz

Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test

The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test is a longer decision-making experience containing 90 survival decisions.

The scenarios cover areas such as food, water, shelter, resources, dangerous encounters, leadership, other survivors, security, planning, and long-term survival.

The test includes a Survival Score and a separate Morality Score. Morality is considered only for decisions where the available choices meaningfully reveal something about values such as compassion, loyalty, responsibility, fairness, or treatment of other people.

Take the Ultimate Survival Test

How the Quizzes Work

The quizzes present fictional situations and several possible choices. There is not necessarily one universally correct answer.

Some decisions may improve your chances of survival while creating difficult consequences for other people. Other choices may prioritize helping others while creating additional risks.

The scoring systems are created specifically for entertainment and interactive storytelling.

Fictional Entertainment

All scenarios on Apocalypse Quizzes are fictional. The quizzes are designed for entertainment and interactive decision-making.

Quiz results should not be considered predictions of how someone would actually behave or perform during a real emergency, disaster, or survival situation.

The quizzes are not professional psychological, personality, military, medical, emergency, or survival assessments.

Future Quizzes

Apocalypse Quizzes is intended to grow into a collection of fictional decision-making experiences. Future quizzes may explore other disaster scenarios, survival situations, and fictional worlds.
