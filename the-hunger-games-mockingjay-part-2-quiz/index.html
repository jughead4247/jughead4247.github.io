About The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2 Movie Quiz

The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2 Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge for fans of the final movie in The Hunger Games series. The film follows Katniss Everdeen as the rebellion reaches its final stage and the fight against the Capitol moves toward its conclusion.

The questions cover Katniss, Squad 451, Boggs, Finnick, Peeta, Pollux, Tigris, President Snow, Coin, Gale, Commander Paylor and major events during the Capitol mission and the aftermath of the rebellion.

Some questions test major plot details, while others focus on smaller facts that are easier to forget. If you remember Katniss's final mission, the Capitol assault and the events surrounding the end of the rebellion, this quiz gives you a chance to test your memory.

How The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2 Movie Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 25 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of the movie based on your performance. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, events, Capitol missions, revelations and final moments featured in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2.
