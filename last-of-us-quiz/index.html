About The Last of Us Quiz

Think you know The Last of Us? Put your memory to the test with this 50-question trivia quiz covering the characters, factions, locations, infected and major events from the HBO series. Whether you have watched the show casually or remember its smaller details, this quiz gives you a chance to find out how well you really know its post-pandemic world.

The questions cover both Seasons 1 and 2, including Joel, Ellie, Tommy, the Fireflies, FEDRA, Jackson, Seattle, the Washington Liberation Front (WLF), Seraphites and the infected. You will encounter questions about important events, character relationships, locations, groups and details from the story.

This is a knowledge quiz rather than a survival simulator. Your goal is simply to remember what happened in the series and choose the correct answer from four possible choices. Some questions are straightforward, while others are designed to test whether you were paying attention to details beyond the major plot points.

How The Last of Us Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions, with four possible answers for each question. Select the answer you believe is correct and continue through the quiz. You can use the navigation buttons to move between questions and review your choices before submitting your final answers.

Every correct answer is worth 2 points, giving you a maximum possible score of 100. Incorrect answers receive 0 points. Your final score is converted into a rank that reflects your level of The Last of Us knowledge.

What Will You Be Tested On?

Expect questions about Joel and Ellie, their journey through the infected world, the people they meet and the choices they make. The quiz also covers major factions such as the Fireflies, FEDRA and the WLF, along with important locations including Jackson and Seattle.

The infected are also part of the quiz, so pay attention to what the series shows about the Cordyceps outbreak and the different dangers created by the infected. If you remember only the biggest moments, some questions may surprise you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions, each with four possible answers.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

Does the quiz cover both seasons?

Yes. The quiz includes characters, events and details from both Seasons 1 and 2 of the HBO series.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Once you finish, use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS option to share your result and see whether someone else can beat your score.
