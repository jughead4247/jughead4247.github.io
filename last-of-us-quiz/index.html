About the Silo Quiz

Think you know everything about Silo? Put your knowledge to the test with this 70-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover characters, departments, locations, relics, the different Silos, major events and important revelations from Seasons 1–3.

Each question has four possible answers. A correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Silo quiz work?

You will face 70 questions about Silo. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum is 70 correct answers. A perfect score of 70 out of 70 is displayed as 100%.

What seasons does the quiz cover?

The quiz covers material from Seasons 1, 2 and 3, including major characters, events and revelations.

What topics are covered?

Questions cover characters, departments, the Silo's history, relics, the Safeguard, Silo 17, Silo 18, Silo 1 and events from the pre-Silo era.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it and try for a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
