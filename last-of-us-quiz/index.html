About The Last of Us Quiz

Think you know The Last of Us? Put your memory to the test with this 50-question knowledge quiz covering the characters, factions, locations, infected and major events from the HBO series. Whether you watched the show casually or paid close attention to its smaller details, this quiz is designed to find out how well you remember the post-pandemic world of The Last of Us.

The quiz covers both Seasons 1 and 2, including Joel, Ellie, Tommy, the Fireflies, FEDRA, Jackson, Seattle, the Washington Liberation Front (WLF), the Seraphites and the infected. Questions can focus on important events, character relationships, groups, locations and details that appeared throughout the story.

This is a knowledge and trivia quiz rather than a survival simulator. Your goal is to remember what happened in the series and select the correct answer from four possible choices. Some questions cover major story moments, while others test whether you noticed smaller details along the way.

How The Last of Us Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions, and every question has four possible answers. Select the answer you believe is correct and use the navigation controls to move through the quiz. You can use the Back button to return to earlier questions and the Next button to continue. Before submitting, you can review your answers using the available navigation.

Each correct answer is worth 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum score of 100 points. Incorrect answers receive 0 points. When you complete all 50 questions, submit your answers to receive your final score and a rank representing your level of The Last of Us knowledge.

Your final result is based on how many questions you answered correctly, so every detail can make a difference. A high score means you have a strong memory of the series, while a lower score may show that it is time for another watch.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ to begin. The quiz displays your current question number and a progress bar so you can see how far you have progressed through the 50 questions. Choose one of the four available answers for each question, then use Next to continue or Back to revisit an earlier question.

After answering the questions, the final submission option becomes available. Complete the quiz and submit your answers to see your score, rank and result description. If you want to play again, select TRY AGAIN after reaching the results screen. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT to share your score or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to invite others to test their knowledge.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions, with four possible answers for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

Does the quiz cover both seasons?

Yes. The questions cover characters, events and details from both Seasons 1 and 2 of the HBO series.

Is the quiz difficult?

The quiz includes both recognizable story moments and questions about smaller details, so casual viewers and dedicated fans may find different questions challenging.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN on the results screen to replay the quiz and try to improve your score.

Can I share my result?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use SHARE MY RESULT to share your result or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to invite friends to beat your score.
