About The Last of Us Quiz

The Last of Us Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge for viewers of the HBO series The Last of Us. It tests how well you remember the characters, events, locations, factions, infected and important details from the post-pandemic story.

The questions cover material from Seasons 1 and 2, including Joel, Ellie, Tommy, the Fireflies, FEDRA, Jackson, Seattle, the Washington Liberation Front (WLF), the Seraphites and the infected. The quiz includes both major story events and smaller details that can be easy to forget.

This quiz is intended for both casual viewers and dedicated fans. Some questions focus on memorable characters and major events, while others test how closely you paid attention to relationships, groups, locations and details revealed throughout the series.

How The Last of Us Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

A correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 50 questions in total, the maximum possible score is 100 points.

Your final score is used to determine your The Last of Us knowledge rank. A higher score means you correctly remembered more details from the series, while a lower score may indicate that some parts of the story deserve another watch.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The quiz will display the first question along with its four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Select the answer you think is correct. Your choice is recorded as you work through the questions, allowing you to review your answers before completing the quiz.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your current question number and progress are shown at the top of the quiz.

Finishing the quiz

Continue answering the questions until you reach the final question. Once all required questions have been answered, the submit option can be used to complete the quiz and view your final result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and challenge other fans to beat your score.

The Last of Us Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions, with four possible choices for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

Which seasons are covered?

The quiz covers Seasons 1 and 2 of the HBO series The Last of Us.

What subjects are included?

Questions cover Joel, Ellie, Tommy, the Fireflies, FEDRA, the WLF, the Seraphites, the infected, locations, major events, character relationships and other details from the series.

Can I replay the quiz?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see who remembers more about The Last of Us.
