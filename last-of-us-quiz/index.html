About The Last of Us Quiz

Think you know The Last of Us? Put your knowledge to the test with this 50-question trivia quiz covering the major characters, locations, factions and events from the HBO series.

Questions cover everything from Joel and Ellie to the Fireflies, Jackson, the infected, Seattle, the WLF and the major events of Seasons 1 and 2.

Each question has four possible answers. A correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. The maximum possible score is 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does The Last of Us Quiz work?

You will face 50 questions about The Last of Us. Each question has four possible answers. Choose the answer you believe is correct and see how much you remember from the series.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

What does my score mean?

Your score determines your rank, ranging from a basic FEDRA casualty to Cordyceps-expert-level knowledge.

Does this quiz cover all seasons?

Yes. The questions cover events and characters from Seasons 1 and 2, including Joel, Ellie, the Fireflies, Jackson, Seattle, the WLF and the infected.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to replay the quiz and try for a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Your Friends button to share your result and see whether your friends can beat your score.
