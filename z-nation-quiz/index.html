About the Z Nation Quiz

Think you know everything about Z Nation? Put your knowledge to the test with this 30-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Murphy, Warren, 10K, Citizen Z, Addy, Garnett, zombies, missions, locations and memorable events from the series.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point. Your final score is converted into a percentage, giving you a maximum possible score of 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Z Nation quiz work?

You will face 30 questions about Z Nation. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100%. Every correct answer is worth 1 point, with the total converted into a percentage.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the characters, zombies, locations, missions and memorable events from Z Nation.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
