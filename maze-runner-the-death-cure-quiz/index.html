About the Maze Runner: The Death Cure Movie Quiz

The Maze Runner: The Death Cure Movie Quiz is a 20-question trivia challenge for fans of the 2018 science-fiction action movie Maze Runner: The Death Cure. The film follows Thomas and the remaining Gladers as they attempt to rescue Minho from WCKD and uncover the truth behind the Immune children.

The questions cover Thomas, Newt, Gally, Jorge, Frypan, Lawrence, Vince, Janson, Ava Paige, WCKD, the Right Arm, the Last City and the final struggle against WCKD.

Some questions test major plot events, while others focus on character details, locations, revelations and important moments from the movie. If you remember the Gladers' final mission and their fight for freedom, this quiz gives you a chance to test your memory.

How the Maze Runner: The Death Cure Movie Quiz Works

The quiz contains 20 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 20 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of the movie based on your performance. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, events, locations and important details featured in Maze Runner: The Death Cure.
