About the Alice in Borderland Season 1 Quiz

Think you know everything about Alice in Borderland Season 1? Put your knowledge to the test with this 25-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Arisu, Usagi, Karube, Chota, the Beach, the deadly games, the playing cards and memorable events from Season 1.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Alice in Borderland quiz work?

You will face 25 questions about Alice in Borderland Season 1. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100%. Every correct answer contributes to your final percentage.

How is the score calculated?

There are 25 questions and each question is worth 1 point. Your number of correct answers is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the characters, games, locations and major events from Alice in Borderland Season 1.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
