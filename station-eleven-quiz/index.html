About the Station Eleven Quiz

Think you know everything about Station Eleven? Put your knowledge to the test with this 30-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Kirsten, Jeevan, Arthur, Miranda, Tyler, the Traveling Symphony, the Museum of Civilization, Severn City Airport and memorable details from the series.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Station Eleven quiz work?

You will face 30 questions about Station Eleven. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100%. Every correct answer contributes to your final percentage.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the series, including its characters, locations, organizations and memorable events.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
