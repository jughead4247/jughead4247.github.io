About the Station Eleven Quiz

The Station Eleven Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge for viewers of the post-pandemic television series Station Eleven. It tests how well you remember the characters, locations, organizations, relationships and memorable events that shape the story.

The questions cover important characters including Kirsten, Jeevan, Arthur, Miranda and Tyler, along with groups and locations such as the Traveling Symphony, the Museum of Civilization and Severn City Airport. The quiz also explores details from the different stages of the story and the world created after the pandemic.

Some questions focus on major characters and events, while others test smaller details that can be easy to forget. Whether you watched Station Eleven recently or remember it from an earlier viewing, this quiz gives you an opportunity to test how much of the series you still remember.

How the Station Eleven Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and choose the answer you believe is correct before moving forward.

Each correct answer contributes to your final score. With 30 questions in total, your result is calculated from the number of questions you answer correctly and converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Your final percentage represents your Station Eleven knowledge level. A high score means you remembered many of the characters, events, locations and details from the series, while a lower score gives you a good reason to revisit the story and test yourself again.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Choose the answer you believe is correct and continue through the quiz.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review your previous selection. Your current question number and progress are displayed during the quiz.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until you have gone through all 30 questions. Once you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and see your final percentage and Station Eleven Knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and challenge other fans.

Station Eleven Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Your final percentage is based on the number of questions you answer correctly.

What does the quiz cover?

Questions cover Kirsten, Jeevan, Arthur, Miranda, Tyler, the Traveling Symphony, the Museum of Civilization, Severn City Airport and memorable details from Station Eleven.

What does my score mean?

Your score shows how many questions you answered correctly and determines your Station Eleven Knowledge level.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your result.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to challenge other fans and see who remembers Station Eleven best.
