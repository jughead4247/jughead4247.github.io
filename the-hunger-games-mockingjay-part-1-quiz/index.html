About the Quiz

The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge for fans of the 2014 science-fiction adventure movie The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1. The film follows Katniss Everdeen as she becomes the symbol of the rebellion against the Capitol and President Snow.

The questions cover Katniss, Peeta, Prim, Finnick, Beetee, Johanna, Cressida, Plutarch, Alma Coin, Boggs, District 13, District 8, District 12, the Capitol and the growing rebellion.

Some questions test major plot events, while others focus on character details, locations, revelations, propaganda missions and important moments from the movie. If you remember Katniss's transformation into the Mockingjay and the rebellion's fight against the Capitol, this quiz gives you a chance to test your memory.

How The Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 25 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of the movie based on your performance. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, events, locations and important details featured in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1.
