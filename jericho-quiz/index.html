About the Jericho Quiz

The Jericho Quiz is a 20-question trivia challenge for fans of the post-apocalyptic television series Jericho. It tests how well you remember the characters, nuclear attacks, organizations, locations and major events that shape the story.

The questions cover important characters including Jake Green, Robert Hawkins, Johnston Green, Eric Green, Stanley Richmond and Emily Sullivan. The quiz also includes Ravenwood, Jennings & Rall, the nuclear attacks and details about the post-nuclear America shown throughout the series.

Some questions focus on major characters and events, while others test details that may be easier to forget. Whether you watched Jericho recently or remember the series from an earlier viewing, this quiz gives you a chance to test how much of its story you still remember.

How the Jericho Quiz Works

The quiz contains 20 questions. Each question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and choose the answer you believe is correct before moving forward.

Every correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Because there are 20 questions, each correct answer contributes 5% to your final result. The maximum possible score is therefore 100%.

Your final percentage represents your Jericho Knowledge level. A high score means you remember many of the characters, events and details surrounding the nuclear attacks and the community of Jericho, while a lower score may mean it is time for another viewing.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Choose the answer you believe is correct and continue through the quiz.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review your previous selection. Your current question number and progress are displayed during the quiz.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until you have gone through all 20 questions. Once you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and see your final percentage and Jericho Knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and challenge other fans.

Jericho Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 20 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer is worth 1 point, and every correct answer contributes 5% to the final percentage.

What does the quiz cover?

Questions cover Jake Green, Robert Hawkins, Johnston Green, Eric Green, Stanley Richmond, Emily Sullivan, Ravenwood, Jennings & Rall, the nuclear attacks and the post-nuclear world of Jericho.

What does my score mean?

Your score shows how many questions you answered correctly and determines your Jericho Knowledge level.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your result.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to challenge other fans and see who knows Jericho best.
