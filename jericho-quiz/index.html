About the Jericho Quiz

Think you know everything about Jericho? Put your knowledge to the test with this 20-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Jake Green, Robert Hawkins, Johnston Green, Eric Green, Stanley Richmond, Emily Sullivan, Ravenwood, Jennings & Rall, the nuclear attacks and the post-nuclear America shown throughout the series.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Jericho quiz work?

You will face 20 questions about Jericho. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100%. Every correct answer contributes 5% to your final percentage.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the series, including its characters, locations, organizations, nuclear attacks and memorable events.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
