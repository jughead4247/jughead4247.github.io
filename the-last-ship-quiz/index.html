About The Last Ship Quiz

Think you know The Last Ship? Put your knowledge to the test with this 50-question trivia quiz covering the major characters, the Nathan James, the Red Flu, factions and major events from the series.

Questions cover everything from Captain Tom Chandler and Mike Slattery to Dr. Rachel Scott, the Red Flu, the Immunes, the Nathan James crew and the major events across all five seasons.

Each question has four possible answers. A correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. The maximum possible score is 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does The Last Ship Quiz work?

You will face 50 questions about The Last Ship. Each question has four possible answers. Choose the answer you believe is correct and see how much you remember from the series.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

What does my score mean?

Your score determines your rank, ranging from a Red Flu casualty to Nathan James-expert-level knowledge.

Does this quiz cover all seasons?

Yes. The questions cover events and characters from all five seasons, including Tom Chandler, Mike Slattery, Rachel Scott, the Nathan James, the Red Flu, the Immunes and the major events of the series.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to replay the quiz and try for a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Your Friends button to share your result and see whether your friends can beat your score.
