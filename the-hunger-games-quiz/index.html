About The Hunger Games Movie Quiz

The Hunger Games Movie Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge based on the 2012 science-fiction action film The Hunger Games. The story follows Katniss Everdeen of District 12 after she volunteers to take her younger sister Prim's place in the 74th Hunger Games.

Katniss enters the Games alongside Peeta Mellark and must survive against tributes from across Panem while navigating the rules and manipulation of the Capitol.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, districts, tributes, locations, major events and memorable details from the 74th Hunger Games.

The Hunger Games Movie Details

Release year: 2012

Director: Gary Ross

Genre: Science fiction, action and adventure

Main setting: District 12, the Capitol and the Hunger Games arena

Main character: Katniss Everdeen, played by Jennifer Lawrence

Key characters: Peeta Mellark, Gale Hawthorne, Haymitch Abernathy and Effie Trinket

Story: Katniss and Peeta compete in the 74th Hunger Games.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in The Hunger Games. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of the Games and events surrounding Katniss.

Characters

Questions may cover Katniss Everdeen, Peeta Mellark, Gale Hawthorne, Primrose Everdeen, Haymitch Abernathy, Effie Trinket, Cinna, Caesar Flickerman, Seneca Crane and other important characters.

Districts and Panem

The quiz includes details about District 12, other districts, the Capitol and the society of Panem.

The Tributes

Questions can test your memory of the tributes, including Cato, Clove, Glimmer, Marvel, Rue, Thresh and Foxface.

The Hunger Games

The quiz covers the 74th Hunger Games, including the Cornucopia, supplies, tracker jackers, alliances, sponsors and major events in the arena.

Capitol and Gamemakers

Questions also cover the Capitol's involvement in the Games, including the Gamemakers, interviews, sponsorships and Seneca Crane.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 30 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, continue to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 30 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers The Hunger Games, including Katniss Everdeen, Peeta Mellark, Gale Hawthorne, Primrose Everdeen, Haymitch, Effie, Cinna, the tributes, District 12, the Capitol, the Gamemakers and major events from the 74th Hunger Games.

Is this quiz only about Katniss?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover the districts, tributes, arena, Capitol, Gamemakers, sponsors and important plot details.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the 2012 science-fiction action film The Hunger Games, directed by Gary Ross and starring Jennifer Lawrence as Katniss Everdeen.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 30 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for The Hunger Games. Questions may reveal important details about Katniss, Peeta, the tributes, the Games, the arena and other major events from the movie.

If you have not watched The Hunger Games, consider watching the movie before taking the quiz to avoid having important story details revealed.
