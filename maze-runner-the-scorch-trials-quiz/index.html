About The Quiz

The Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge based on the science-fiction survival movie The Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials. The story follows Thomas and the other Gladers after they escape the Maze and enter the dangerous Scorch.

The movie expands the story beyond the Maze as Thomas, Newt, Minho, Teresa and the other survivors discover more about WCKD and the Flare. Their journey takes them through abandoned cities, dangerous settlements and areas controlled by people trying to survive the outbreak.

This quiz focuses on characters, locations, organizations, Cranks, the Right Arm and major events shown throughout The Scorch Trials. Some questions test major plot points while others focus on smaller details that can be easy to forget.

Movie Details

Release year: 2015

Director: Wes Ball

Genre: Science fiction, action, thriller and dystopian survival

Main setting: The Scorch, an abandoned and dangerous region affected by the Flare

Main character: Thomas, played by Dylan O'Brien

Main organization: WCKD, the organization investigating and exploiting immunity to the Flare

Major threat: The Cranks and the dangers created by the Flare

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in The Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of specific locations, organizations and major events.

Characters

Questions may cover Thomas, Teresa, Newt, Minho, Frypan, Winston, Aris, Brenda, Jorge, Janson, Mary Cooper and other characters who play important roles in the story.

WCKD

WCKD remains central to the story after the Gladers escape the Maze. The quiz includes questions about WCKD facilities, personnel, experiments and its pursuit of the immune survivors.

The Scorch

The Scorch is the dangerous environment through which Thomas and the other survivors travel. Questions may test your memory of the abandoned cities, ruined buildings, settlements and other locations encountered during their journey.

The Cranks

The Cranks are people affected by the Flare and are one of the major dangers faced by the survivors. The quiz includes questions about their behavior, appearances and encounters throughout the movie.

The Right Arm

The Right Arm is a resistance organization opposing WCKD. Questions may cover its members, activities, leadership and role in the events of the movie.

Major Story Events

The quiz covers important events from the Gladers' escape from WCKD through their journey across the Scorch and their encounters with the Cranks, survivors, resistance members and WCKD.

How The Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 25 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 25 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, continue to the next question.

Finishing the Quiz

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 25 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers The Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials, including Thomas, Teresa, Newt, Minho, Aris, Brenda, Jorge, Janson, WCKD, the Scorch, Cranks, the Right Arm, the Flare and major story events.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover WCKD, the Scorch, Cranks, the Right Arm, locations, the Flare and important plot details.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on The Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials, the second film in The Maze Runner movie series.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 25 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
